Automatic reminder delivery

The Google Apps Script sends daily AMC status and expiry reminders to all six nominated recipients: rithikeshrathi@bharatpetroleum.in, gauravmadan@bharatpetroleum.in, patilvishalravi@bharatpetroleum.in atharavsanjayka@bharatpetroleum.in, rithikeshrathi3117@gmail.com and anandrao@bharatpetroleum.in.

After expiry, a reminder is sent every day: −1, −2, −3, −4 days overdue, and daily thereafter.

Two independent Apps Script triggers check the shared AMC register daily at approximately 8:00 AM India time: one sends the mandatory daily status email every day, even when no AMC is expiring; the other sends expiry-specific reminders on configured reminder dates and every day after expiry.
